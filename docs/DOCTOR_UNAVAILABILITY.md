# Staff-managed full-day doctor unavailability

Implemented 18 October 2026. Staff manages future full-day absences through Doctor schedules -> choose doctor -> Full-day unavailability -> date/reason -> Review -> Confirm. Dates must be after Malaysia today. The reason (up to300 characters) is shown only to staff and the assigned doctor; patient availability and errors never include it. This increment does not edit/remove absences, manage partial days, or automatically cancel/reschedule visits. The confirmation explains these limits.

Adding an absence rejects any non-cancelled booking whose slot window overlaps that Malaysia calendar day, including an overnight slot starting the previous day. Waiting, called and completed records are protected; only cancelled history allows the absence. The rejection changes no records and tells staff to resolve the appointments first. No reason, booking or clinical history is rewritten.

Once saved:

- Existing empty or cancelled-history slots remain stored with their original `closed` state, but overlapping slots disappear from public availability. Managed slot lists and the doctor's read-only weekly schedule label them Doctor unavailable. The doctor sees an all-day banner and the saved reason, even if no slots exist.
- Direct patient, staff counter and follow-up bookings, patient/staff rescheduling and new manual slots recheck the absence in their existing serialized transactions. Stale screens cannot bypass it. Exact retries of already-saved bookings/slots remain historical retries, not new work.
- Weekly preview marks affected proposed times Skip unavailable. Confirmation skips them while generating the other non-conflicting days; adding an absence after preview invalidates the old fingerprint. All original weekly conflict/atomicity/idempotency rules remain. Historical generation retry results still describe the original batch and do not recreate blocked slots.
- Startup demo seeding respects absent dates. Other doctors and non-overlapping dates remain unaffected. Midnight boundaries use half-open instant comparisons in Malaysia time, so a slot ending exactly at midnight before the absence is allowed, while one crossing into it is blocked.

Persistence: additive `doctor_unavailability` table, unique doctor/date and request key. Staff-only GET/POST `/staff/schedules/{doctor_id}/unavailability`; immutable reason/date, exact request retry safe and changed reuse rejected. Creation and competing booking writes use `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`. Doctor schedule derives identity from the actor and returns only their absences. Real authentication remains deferred; demo headers are selectable identities.
